Privacy Policy

Orbit Defense · Last updated: 30 September 2026

Orbit Defense ("the game") is made by The Beer Doctor, an independent developer based in Greece ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Email thebeerdoctorgames@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't create an account with me, and I don't run any servers that store your data.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad Privacy.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, upgrades, levels, stars, skins, settings, statistics such as enemies destroyed) in a file on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game.

2. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the game with your Play Games profile (player name and avatar) and stores, in your Google account:

  • a cloud copy of your save file (so it can be restored after a reinstall or on another device),
  • your best Endless score for the public leaderboard, and
  • the achievements you unlock.

This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete your Play Games data for this game in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The game receives a confirmation of the purchase (product, order and purchase token) so it can deliver the item and restore it later. I do not receive your name, address or payment information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Players without Premium can choose to watch rewarded video ads (for example to revive or double their coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your IP address (used to estimate your general location, e.g. country),
  • information about ads you see and interact with, and
  • technical and diagnostic information (device model, OS version, app performance).

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalise ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland you will see Google's consent form before any ad is loaded, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad Privacy. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying Premium removes ads completely.

5. Analytics

The game does not currently use a separate analytics service. If this changes, this policy will be updated before it happens.

7. Sharing

I do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Play, Google Play Games, Google AdMob), which may process it outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Device data: until you uninstall the game.
  • Play Games data: until you delete it in your Google account or Play Games settings.
  • Ad data: according to Google's retention policies.

Because I don't store your data on my own servers, most requests are fastest handled through the settings above. You can also email me and I'll help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact me at thebeerdoctorgames@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

10. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact me and it will be deleted.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
